How I work

Observation first

Any problem can be solved through design — it's a matter of keen observation and meaningful research into the people we're designing for.

Data and intuition

Data reveals what is happening; empathy explains why. I trust both, and treat them as partners rather than opposing forces.

Collaboration over silos

The best ideas rarely come from working alone. I thrive in co-creation with engineers, PMs, and stakeholders, where different perspectives sharpen the outcome.

Always evolving

A lifelong learner — constantly exploring new tools, trends, and practices to keep solutions fresh and future-ready.
