Redesigning the Poker Game Table

A cleaner, more intuitive redesign of the poker game table that reduces clutter and cognitive load, delivering a more immersive player experience.

RoleSenior Product Designer
FocusInteraction & visual design
Frameworks usedGestalt, Fitts's Law, Hick's Law

Introduction

The game table is the core experience of any online card game — it's where players spend most of their time, so it has to be intuitive, visually clear, and engaging.

The old design was functional but cluttered, inconsistent, and behind modern usability standards. The redesign aimed to create a cleaner, more immersive, and user-friendly table that improves engagement and reduces cognitive load.

Research & problem identification

Through surveys, in-game feedback, and stakeholder input, multi-tabling professionals in particular reported poor readability of core gameplay elements. Five priorities emerged for where players' attention actually goes:

  • Cards — the most critical element for decision-making; players need instant clarity on their own hand and the shared cards.
  • Pot value — visibility of the total pot to evaluate risk and reward each hand.
  • Bets — reading self and opponent bet amounts to track the flow of action.
  • Table balance — chip stacks at a glance, vital for strategy in multi-table play.
  • Username — identifying opponents by name to support quick recognition in fast-paced games.

Competitive analysis

Junglee Poker was benchmarked against Natural8 and PokerBaazi across visual design, layout and ergonomics, interactivity, feature availability, and performance. The pattern was consistent: competitors led on visual distinctiveness, clear information hierarchy, and rich feedback, while Junglee's table felt cluttered and its player information hard to parse.

Design goals

  • Simplify the interface by reducing clutter.
  • Improve visual hierarchy for better decision-making.
  • Enhance usability of key actions — Fold, Call, Raise.
  • Create a modern, premium aesthetic aligned with player expectations.
  • Ensure accessibility: readability, color contrast, scalable UI.

Design principles applied

Gestalt principles

People naturally perceive visual elements as organised groups rather than individual pieces.

Grouped player name, chips, and avatar into a single clear unit, cutting clutter and improving scannability.

Fitts's Law

Time to reach a target depends on its size and distance — bigger, closer targets are faster to hit.

Fold, Call, and Raise were enlarged and moved to the bottom for quick reach, especially on mobile.

Hick's Law

More choices mean slower decisions — fewer, clearer options speed things up.

Simplified betting to preset sizes plus a clear slider, cutting overwhelm and speeding gameplay.

Before & after

Before

Redundant labels, cluttered chip placement, cropped avatars, and uneven line spacing left players questioning the table's polish.

After

A streamlined player layout shows only essentials — name, stack, action.

Impact: players scan the table instantly, with no cognitive overload.

Before

Action buttons had inconsistent colors, low contrast, irregular sizing, and blended into the background.

After

Larger, high-contrast buttons in standardized colors — red for Fold, green for Call, orange for Raise — placed for easy thumb reach.

Impact: faster decisions, fewer misclicks.

Before

Hole cards got lost in the clutter, were placed unrealistically on the table, and were too small to read clearly.

After

Centralised cards with clear numerals and improved stacking visuals for a realistic, premium feel.

Impact: easier to track cards and bets, more confidence in gameplay.

Before

Turn timers covered player info, status was hard to track, and floating emoji icons felt disconnected from the profile.

After

High-visibility status tags (All-in, Check, Disconnected), a glowing active-player highlight, and descriptive winning-hand labels.

Impact: stronger situational awareness, less confusion, more immersion.

Impact & business value

Improved engagement

Players spend more time on tables thanks to better usability.

Reduced drop-offs

A cleaner layout makes it easier for new players to adapt.

Brand perception

An elevated design aligns with modern gaming standards, increasing trust and appeal.

Final outcome

The redesign transformed the game table into a modern, intuitive, immersive experience. The new interface looks premium while improving usability and reducing cognitive load — letting players focus on strategy rather than fighting the interface.
