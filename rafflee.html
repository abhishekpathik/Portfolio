Designing Rafflee, a UK Prize Draw Platform

Rafflee is a real-money prize-draw and competitions platform built for the UK market — designed and shipped from scratch, from account creation through to entering a draw and winning.

RoleProduct Designer
Scope0 → 1, web, mobile web & app
LaunchedNovember 2025

Overview

Rafflee lets people enter prize draws for cars, watches, holidays, and cash — priced per ticket, with instant wins, subscriptions, and standalone raffles running side by side.

As a ground-up build, the project spanned three core flows: player account management, draw creation, and ticket bundling — designed to work across desktop web, mobile web, and native app from day one, while reusing existing backend services wherever possible to move faster.

Designing for trust, not just conversion

UK prize competitions sit under specific legal requirements — namely, offering a genuinely free, equal-chance way to enter, alongside the paid ticket route. That constraint shaped several core design decisions rather than being an afterthought bolted on later.

  • Free postal entry is surfaced directly on every draw, not buried in the terms — visible on the listing, the product page, and the entry flow itself.
  • A tax-free cash alternative is always shown alongside the prize, so the offer is transparent about real value.
  • A guaranteed giveaway promise — prizes are always awarded regardless of entry count — is stated upfront as a trust signal, not hidden in a FAQ.

The product page surfaces free postal entry, ticket tiers, live sold%, and a personal spend limit — all above the fold.

Browsing & entering a draw

Draws are split into distinct types — Mega competitions, Instant wins, Sit 'n Go, and standalone raffles — so a first-time visitor isn't stuck parsing one undifferentiated list.

Rafflee+ subscriptions

Subscriptions bundle recurring entries across Mega, Instant Win, and Lifestyle competitions into three simple tiers — designed to make the value-per-ticket improvement obvious at a glance rather than something a user has to calculate themselves.

  • Three tiers only — kept deliberately simple rather than offering an overwhelming number of plans.
  • Each tier shows its per-ticket value improvement directly next to the entry count, so the upgrade case makes itself.
  • "Cancel at any time" is stated upfront, next to the pricing — addressing the most common subscription hesitation before it's even asked.

Checkout & the moment after purchase

Checkout skips a cart entirely — clicking "Enter now" or "Subscribe" goes straight to payment, since almost every purchase here is a single, immediate decision rather than something people compare across multiple items.

Same confirmation pattern carried through to mobile web, with purchased tickets visible immediately.

Account & responsible gaming

A logged-in player's account brings together tickets, prizes, transactions, and settings in one place — with responsible gaming treated as a first-class section, not a link buried in a footer.

"Responsible gaming" sits alongside My Tickets and Payment Settings — same visual weight as any other account function.

Key design decisions

Guest-first checkout

No forced sign-up before entering a draw — purchase first, with account creation folded in only where it's actually needed.

Distinct draw formats

Mega, Instant Win, Sit 'n Go, and standalone raffles are visually and structurally separated, so the browsing experience doesn't blur into one long list.

Trust built into the UI

Free entry, cash alternatives, and the guaranteed giveaway promise are surfaced on the page itself — not left for a terms & conditions link.

Simple subscription tiers

Three plans only, each with a visible per-ticket value gain — designed to sell itself without needing an explainer.

Responsible gaming, first-class

Spend limits are visible directly on the product page, and a dedicated Responsible Gaming section sits in the main account navigation.

Outcome

Rafflee shipped as a complete 0-to-1 product in November 2025 — account management, draw creation, ticket bundling, subscriptions, and a full web, mobile web, and app experience, designed and built from an empty canvas.

Since launch, the platform has run a continuous stream of live competitions across cars, watches, holidays, and cash prizes, paid out real winners shown transparently on its own winners feed, and built a recurring subscriber base through Rafflee+.
